# Data Journey — User Guide

Welcome to **Data Journey**, your personal offline tracker for master learning curves, software projects, and daily productivity.

---

## 1. Dashboard Overview
- **Header Actions**: Quick access to start a live study stopwatch or record today's reflection.
- **Metric KPIs**: Real-time counts of your Active Projects, Total Hours Logged, Current & Longest Daily Streak, and Task Completion Velocity.
- **Current Project Card**: Visual progress bar showing actual hours vs planned target hours, along with your schedule health status (`Ahead of Schedule`, `On Track`, `At Risk`, or `Behind`).
- **Today's Focus**: Instantly see if you've logged your daily session today to keep your streak intact.
- **Consistency Heatmap**: A GitHub-style daily grid showing your dedication and intensity over the last 10 weeks.
- **Recent Activity Feed**: The latest chronological milestones, reflections, and completed tasks.

---

## 2. Managing Projects
1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Click **+ New Project** to set up a new journey:
   - **Type**: Learning/Course, Software Development, Portfolio, Research, or Business.
   - **Target Days & Hours**: Enter your timeline (e.g. 35 days, 1 hour/day, 35 hours total).
3. Click **Set as Active** on any card to switch the dashboard and tracking context to that project.

---

## 3. Logging Daily Progress
1. Click **Daily Progress** in the sidebar (or **+ Log Day** in the top header).
2. Enter the Day Number (e.g., Day 1, Day 2 ... Day 35), date, and section worked on.
3. Fill out the structured reflection prompts:
   - **Daily Goal**: What you set out to accomplish.
   - **What I Learned**: Summaries, syntax, key ideas, and insights.
   - **💡 Breakthrough / Win**: Fast solutions, concepts that clicked, or milestones.
   - **⚠️ Challenge / Problem**: Error messages or blockers you faced.
4. Click **Save Daily Progress**. Any breakthrough or issue entered is automatically cross-referenced in your Roadblocks & Wins board!

---

## 4. Tasks & Goals
- **Tasks**: Create specific tasks (e.g., "Solve 10 SQL queries", "Write data cleaning pipeline"). Mark them complete with a single click.
- **Goals & Milestones**: Track higher-level milestones (e.g., "Complete Capstone Dataset Preparation") with an interactive percentage progress slider.

---

## 5. Live Study Stopwatch & Time Tracking
1. Open **Time Tracking** from the navigation.
2. Click **▶ Start** on the stopwatch when you sit down for a focused study or coding block.
3. When finished, click **✓ Log Session** to automatically record the exact minutes spent directly into your project's time log.

---

## 6. Calendar & Deadlines
- The **Calendar** provides an interactive monthly overview:
  - Green dots signify days where you hit your 1-hour study target.
  - Amber dots indicate partial study days.
  - Purple tags mark due dates for tasks and goals.
- Click on any day cell to view or edit the exact records for that date!

---

## 7. Backups, Export & Data Ownership
- Open **Data & Backups** in the sidebar.
- Click **Download JSON Backup** to save a complete file (`data-journey-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json`) to your computer.
- To restore or transfer to another computer, use **Select Backup File to Import...** and choose either **Full Restore** or **Smart Merge**.
